## Methodology Note

This tracker uses a **subjective Bayesian** approach — not a model output. Key rules:

1. **Scenarios are exhaustive and mutually exclusive** (sum to 100%).
2. **P(open|scenario)** is set based on whether that scenario's resolution removes both the US blockade AND IRGC interference to ~pre-war commercial traffic levels.
3. **30-day window** runs from today's date (2026-05-26).
4. **Estimate updates when new signal arrives** — it does not automatically decay. Absence of news is weak evidence of stalemate (slightly bearish for reopening). Calendar compression of scenario feasibility is treated as a passive structural factor, not an explicit signal, until a threshold is crossed (the first such threshold, the partial-arrangement cliff, fired May 20 on schedule).
5. **Confidence in the point estimate is low** — the range is roughly ±8 percentage points given scenario probability uncertainty.
6. **Post-revision regime (May 25 onward):** Rule B (all-into-stalemate) is the live primary read; Rules A and C are tracked as comparators through May 28, after which the comparator readings are dropped and Rule B becomes the single live ladder.

## Executive Bottom Line
- **Ballistic missile threat:** Degraded but still real. Iran can still generate meaningful daily salvos, especially into Gulf targets, but at sharply reduced volume versus opening days.
- **Drone threat:** More durable than the ballistic threat. Deep reserves plus likely Russian support make drone pressure the long-war problem.

- **Primary coalition problem:** Defending Gulf energy / civilian infrastructure against persistent drone and reduced-but-steady missile harassment.

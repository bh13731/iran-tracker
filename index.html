      The Reconstitution Plateau: 17 Days, ~200 Launchers, Three Explanations
      May 10, 2026
      Day 33. P(Hormuz reopen 30d) = ~15%, static for 12 consecutive days. Iran’s effective launcher count has held at ~200 for 17 days — since the ISW Apr 19 baseline that revised the figure up from ~102 after underground reconstitution. No subsequent authoritative source has moved the number in either direction. Three structurally different explanations are consistent with the plateau: the underground reserve is fully tapped, Iran is deliberately pacing further extraction, or intelligence reporting has plateaued without the operational reality having stabilized. Each carries materially different implications for rupture-risk profile, US carrier posture, and Iran’s reservation point in any partial Hormuz arrangement.
